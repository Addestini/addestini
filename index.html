The Real Problem

Still Stuck Under ₹1 Lakh?
Here's What's Actually Holding You Back

It's not the platform. It's not your closing skills. Most of the time, it comes down to one of these five things.

You're depending on platforms you can't control.

Instagram reach changes overnight. Group posts get you banned. Bought lead lists give you junk. When your lead source is outside your control, so is your income — and scaling becomes impossible.

Your ad angle is speaking to the wrong person.

Most affiliates write ads about their product — not about the buyer's problem. If the first line of your ad doesn't immediately feel personal, the right people keep scrolling and the wrong ones click.

Your landing page or funnel is doing too little.

If someone clicks your ad and lands somewhere that doesn't immediately build trust and push them to act — you've already lost them. A weak funnel wastes ad spend faster than anything else.

Warm leads are going cold before you reach them.

People who clicked your ad were interested — for about 20 minutes. Without a retargeting system and a fast follow-up flow, those leads move on. The pipeline leaks and you start from scratch each month.

You're too involved in lead generation to focus on closing.

When you're the one posting, running ads, chasing leads, and closing — nothing gets done well. The affiliates crossing ₹1L+ consistently have one thing in common: they've handed off the lead side entirely.
