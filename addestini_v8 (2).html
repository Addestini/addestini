The Real Problem

Still Stuck Under ₹1 Lakh?
Here's What's Actually Holding You Back

It's not the platform. It's not your closing skills. Most of the time, it comes down to one of these five things.

🔗

Depending on platforms you can't control

Instagram reach drops. Groups get you banned. Bought leads are junk. No control over source = no control over income.

🎯

Ad angle speaking to the wrong person

You're writing about your product, not the buyer's problem. The right people scroll past. The wrong ones click.

📉

Weak funnel burning your ad budget

Someone clicks — then lands on a page that doesn't build trust. They leave. Every rupee spent on that click is gone.

⏱️

Warm leads going cold before you reach them

Interested leads wait 20 minutes — not 2 days. No retargeting or fast follow-up means the pipeline leaks every month.

😵

Too busy generating leads to focus on closing

You can't post, run ads, chase leads, and close well — all at once. Affiliates crossing ₹1L+ hand off the lead side entirely.

Absolutely. You don't need any prior experience with ads. We handle everything from scratch — campaign setup, targeting, creatives, copy, and optimization. Just share your affiliate platform and budget, and we take it from there.

We recommend starting with ₹5,000–₹10,000/month in ad spend. This gives enough data to test, optimize, and find what converts. The more consistent your spend, the faster we scale.
